The guides

Two handbooks ship with the game. Start with the pond guide; come back to the genetics guide when a clutch surprises you.

The Pond Guide

How the whole game fits together: your first day, a duck's life, running the flock, money, competition, the rival ponds, the Society, the calendar, the long game, and the companion, keyboard, and settings.

The Genetics Guide

Every locus, dominance rule, and show standard; inheritance, mutation, and inbreeding; rarity and pedigree; where the rare genes come from; and breeding recipes.
